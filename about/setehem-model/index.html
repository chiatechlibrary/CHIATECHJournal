Journal framework

The SETEHEM Model

A connected pathway from scientific understanding to education, technological application, engineering design, ethical social value, entrepreneurial conversion and responsible management.

ScienceEducationTechnologyEngineeringHumanitiesEntrepreneurshipManagement

From theory to practical impact

CHIATECH JOURNAL publishes scholarship that can be understood, tested, taught, implemented, evaluated and improved. The model treats disciplines as connected stages rather than isolated silos.

Knowledge

Science investigates nature and generates evidence.

Learning

Education translates evidence into human capability.

Application

Technology applies knowledge to practical tasks.

Design

Engineering organises resources to create dependable solutions.

Responsibility

Humanities examines meaning, values, equity and social consequences.

Value creation

Entrepreneurship converts innovation into viable and scalable ventures.

Delivery

Management aligns teams, resources, strategy, quality and sustainability.

CHIATECH JOURNAL

Bridging theory and practical application through rigorous, ethical and useful scholarship.
