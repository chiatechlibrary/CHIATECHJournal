Aims & Scope

Broad enough for interdisciplinary work, structured enough to maintain editorial coherence.

CHIATECH JOURNAL

Aims

To advance rigorous and practically useful knowledge; strengthen research communication; encourage collaboration across disciplines; amplify context-aware scholarship from Africa and beyond; and support the translation of evidence into technology, education, enterprise, policy and social value.

Scope

Science

Experimental, natural, health and environmental sciences.

Technology

Computing, AI, data, cybersecurity and digital innovation.

Engineering

Design, systems, energy, manufacturing and infrastructure.

Mathematics

Pure, applied, statistics, modelling and computation.

Education

STEM pedagogy, assessment, curriculum and learning technologies.

Humanities & Social Sciences

Human-centred research connecting STEM, society, policy and culture.

Entrepreneurship & Management

Innovation, enterprise, operations, finance and sustainable development.

Article types

  • Original research articles
  • Systematic, scoping, narrative and critical reviews
  • Technical and practice articles
  • Case studies and field reports
  • Short communications
  • Data, methods and software notes
  • STEM education innovations
  • Policy perspectives and scholarly commentaries
  • Book, tool and resource reviews
  • Editorials, corrections and retraction notices

Out of scope

Manuscripts without a clear scholarly contribution, unverifiable claims, fabricated data, copied content, purely promotional material, or work outside the journal’s ethical and methodological expectations will not proceed to review.
