Journal policy

Originality, Plagiarism & AI Policy

Requirements for originality, responsible overlap and disclosed AI-assisted work.

CHIATECH JOURNAL

Originality

Submissions must be original and not simultaneously under review elsewhere. Properly cited preprints, theses and conference versions must be disclosed.

Text overlap

Similarity software may support editorial assessment, but a percentage alone does not determine misconduct. Editors evaluate the nature, location, citation and significance of overlap.

Generative AI

  • AI tools cannot be authors.
  • Authors must disclose substantive AI use and remain accountable for every claim, citation, image and dataset.
  • AI-generated or modified images must be clearly identified and must not misrepresent evidence.
  • Confidential, personal or proprietary information must not be entered into public AI systems.
  • Reviewers and editors must not upload confidential manuscripts into public AI tools.
