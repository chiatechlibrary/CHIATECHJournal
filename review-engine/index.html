Evidence model

What the engine reviews

Deterministic checks are mapped to journal policy and internationally recognised reporting and publication-integrity principles.

01

Journal alignment

Domain fit, problem statement, contribution and practical relevance.

02

Reporting completeness

Article-specific structure and signals for CONSORT, PRISMA, STROBE, CARE, COREQ, ARRIVE, CHEERS or related guidance.

03

Reference integrity

In-text/reference matching, DOI extraction and live Crossref metadata verification.

04

Originality signals

Internal repeated-sequence analysis and prior-title metadata matches for human follow-up.

05

AI-use transparency

Disclosure and model-signature signals without presenting unreliable style inference as proof.

06

Editorial routing

A readiness score plus distinct action lists for the author and qualified human editors.
