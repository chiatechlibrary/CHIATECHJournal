Event Decoration as a Source of Household Income Sustainability in Garkawa, Plateau State, Nigeria: A Descriptive Survey

Source: Article title in approved manuscript

Article focus

Background: Event decoration is an increasingly visible creative-service microenterprise in Nigeria and can diversify household livelihoods through income generated from weddings, birthdays, corporate functions, religious events and other ceremonies.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Research context

Purpose: This study examined the demographic profile, service portfolio, income contribution and operating constraints of practising event decorators in Garkawa, Plateau State, Nigeria.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Study approach

Methods: A descriptive cross-sectional survey was conducted among 50 practising event decorators using a structured questionnaire.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Reported findings

Data were analysed with frequencies, percentages and arithmetic means.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Results: Seventy-six percent of respondents were aged 20–40 years, 56% were women, 56% had secondary education and 24% had tertiary education.
