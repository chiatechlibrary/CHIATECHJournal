CHIATECH JOURNAL explanatory summary

Church Engagement and Perceived Reduction of Community Insecurity in Abuja, Nigeria: Survey-Weighted Evidence Across the Six FCT Area Councils

Source: Article title in approved manuscript

Article focus

Background: Religious institutions are socially influential in Nigeria, but their public-security contribution should be measured as specific practices rather than presumed from visibility or doctrine.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Research context

Objective: To estimate the relationship between five dimensions of Church engagement and perceived reduction of community insecurity (PRCI) across the Federal Capital Territory.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Study approach

Methods: A stratified multistage household survey generated 586 response records from 642 field contacts in 24 clusters.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Reported findings

The CECIQ measured TPEMF, MDPA, SEHPC, CSEW, IIC and PRCI.

Source: Abstract in approved manuscript

Interpretation

Weighted Spearman correlations and a survey-weighted, cluster-robust multiple regression with prespecified covariates and Holm correction were used.
