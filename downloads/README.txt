AUTHORSHIP POLICY ADOPTED
There is no universal scholarly rule that sets one numeric maximum author count across all disciplines. CHIATECH JOURNAL therefore adopts a practical standard byline maximum of 12 individually named authors. Every author must meet the journal’s authorship criteria and have a transparent contribution record. Genuine collaborations with more than 12 qualifying authors may be approved by the editor using group/collaborative authorship or the dedicated continuation sheet. No qualifying author should be removed merely to meet the standard byline limit.

COPYRIGHT / LICENCE POLICY ADOPTED
Authors retain copyright unless another legal rights holder applies. Authors grant CHIA TECH SOLUTIONS AND RESOURCES LIMITED the right of first publication and non-exclusive rights needed to publish, distribute, archive, index and maintain the version of record. The journal’s default open-access scholarly licence is CC BY 4.0 unless an article-specific approved exception is recorded.
